Read the message

Someone invites you to collaborate, asks a question, or needs a decision. Start with the email in front of you.

Step 02

Choose your intent

Agree, decline politely, ask for more information, request time, suggest a meeting, or keep the door open.

Step 03

Review and respond

Get a draft to edit, personalize, and copy. You stay in control of the final message and when it gets sent.
